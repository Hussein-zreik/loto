What this tool can and cannot do. It cannot improve your chance of matching the draw — that is fixed at 1 in 5,245,786 for any set of six numbers, and this archive's own draws test as statistically fair (χ²=49.0 vs. 56.9 critical), so "hot" and "overdue" numbers carry no predictive signal whatsoever. What it can do is raise your expected payout: jackpots are split between all winners, and human picks cluster hard on birthdays, patterns and sequences. Choosing an uncrowded combination means that if you do win, you are far less likely to split the prize.

Rules — ★ = recommended, on by default

Why only the ★ rules are on. Rules cost you nothing — a single ticket is 1 in 5,245,786 whichever combination it is, so narrowing the pool does not narrow your chance. But only some rules buy you anything. The starred ones steer away from combinations humans over-pick (birthdays, runs, slip patterns, replayed past winners), which cuts the risk of splitting a prize. The unstarred ones — sum ranges, odd/even balance, spread — filter real draws at exactly the same rate they filter random ones (verified against all 2,421 draws in this archive), so they target nothing. They are harmless; they are just not worth switching on.
